A live map of where the guild's players are in Old School RuneScape. For members of the guild only: log in with the Discord account you use on the guild's hub. If you have never signed in to the hub, do that first.

You log in at discord.com. The map reads your Discord ID and name, never your email, messages or servers.

The map never asks for your RuneScape or Jagex login. It shows only players who paired the RuneLite plugin with the guild's hub, and only what they share there: a location kept private on the hub is not shown.
